## 기본 시트 프롬프트

> Make a new side-view boss sprite sheet for an original winter tower action game. The one attached tiny 12F game sprite is a reference for production pixel-art discipline, tilt, and scale. The new boss is an imprisoned executioner. Use real, sharply stepped, low-resolution game pixel art, approximately 40–43 occupied native pixels high on an 80×80 cell. Imagine pixel-perfect nearest-neighbor enlarging of original Aseprite artwork. The design must read from three silhouettes: (1) a large SQUARE, brick-rust hammer head on a prominent straight light brown handle, held forward to the left, with a dark outer outline and clear transparent separation from the torso wherever possible; (2) a peaked bone-silver hood above the shoulders, with large PITCH BLACK face opening and THREE wide light iron bars set in the black face opening, like a prison grille, visibly distinct from all clothing; (3) a large warm copper wrist manacle with a square bright buckle and a short dangling open U-shaped ring separated from the dark sleeve. Add a small rust-red neck strip. Make the shoulders and body as a single dark slate cloak and broad angular light forms, then down to two distinct black boots. Crisp thick ink outer outline, clear contrast, 8 to 10 flat color swatches only: pitch charcoal ink, dark slate, medium blue slate, warm light stone, ivory edge, rust red and warm copper/lighter metal. Large SOLID unbroken shapes, two hard-edged shades per material. Deliberately no tiny texture, scratches, flecks, speckle, grain, stipple, soft lighting, gradients, shiny reflections, dither, AA, or picture-like brush. Distinct asymmetrical memorable graphic silhouette. Do not put the hammer against the ground on idle: stable body stance, shaft held diagonally, block head held in free air forward at hip level, visually unmistakable as a hammer. The three motifs must survive downscaling and remain visibly discrete in every pose. Sprite always faces left, lateral side-view. Actual alpha transparency.
>
> Sheet is exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of regular square cells with ample transparent outer margins and large unbroken transparent gutters; keep full hammer head and boots in each cell. Same body scale, same accessories, same matching feet baseline. No in-image labels, letters, glyphs, checkerboard, floor, shadow or FX. Row 1: readable idle showing all 3 motifs; windup with hammer drawn up; approaching step with hammer visible; light contact with squared hammer driven far LEFT. Row 2: swing follow, recoil to ready for second attack, second light contact thrust at different height, guarded pause with hammer visible. Row 3: light hit flinch, stronger hit stagger, kneeling knockdown with one knee bending and the hammer still visible, fully collapsed low on the ground. Row 4: two separated advancing walk steps; compact rear idle still showing hammer and bars; compact rear warning. One isolated complete sprite per cell. Graphic sprites for a live game at native pixel scale, not a textured illustration.
