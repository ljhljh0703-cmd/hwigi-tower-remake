## 생성 프롬프트

### 돌진

> Create one production sprite sheet of a NEW enemy for an existing 2D pixel-art roguelike, in a winter ruin tower. The attached seal-sentinel-idle.png is a style reference only. Match its tiny hand-placed pixel clusters, charcoal outline, gray weathered stone surfaces and warm gray highlights. Distinct new creature: a feral squat quadruped assembled by the tower from slate stair fragments, with one wedge-shaped forepaw and a dime-sized oxidized rust-red seam embedded at its shoulder. Low long head, strong forelegs, black-gray back separated from the outline, color limited to slate gray, a few light stone shades, charcoal, and subdued rusty red. The creature looks left on every frame. Show a strict side view at game eye level, the same creature, markings and proportions across all poses.
>
> Asset layout: perfectly regular 4 columns × 4 rows, 16 independent sprites, one centered in each equal square cell. Each isolated sprite must fit fully inside its cell with generous clear gutters, fully transparent space around the body. Baseline consistent in every cell, same size and silhouette weight. Source pixel style constrained as if painted on native 48×48 per tile and then scaled with crisp nearest-neighbor blocks. Large clean coherent pixel clusters, no feathering, gradients, anti-alias, painterly detail, checkerboard, ground, scenery, shadow, captions, UI, symbols, lines, or padding marks. Transparent background must be actual alpha.
>
> Frame order left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Row 1: idle low alert stance; crouched anticipation with drawn-back forepaw; lunge approach; extended left forepaw contact. Row 2: follow-through with paw down; braced second wind-up; second forepaw contact with different stride; core crouched for a burst. Row 3: light recoil; heavy backward recoil; forelegs buckled; collapsed stone creature at ground level. Row 4: advancing plant pose; advancing step pose; tight rear-row idle with compact paws; rear-row lowered attack anticipation. No text anywhere.

### 방패와 호위

> Production video game sprite sheet with transparent background, 4 by 4 equal square cells, all 16 cells used. Original NEW shield sentry from a winter ruin tower. The attached shield-soldier-idle.png is only a style guide for native coarse pixel clusters, charcoal outline, desaturated gray and deep oxidized rust. Design a taller bipedal sentinel whose body looks like fitted gray masonry armor. It has a dark square slit visor, one broken stone shoulder corner and a large slab shield with a distinct stepped top, vertical iron spine and restrained brick-red rust bands. Same character and shield across all poses. Short weapon free arm behind the shield. The pose and silhouette must read at native 48×48 logical pixels per cell. All sprites face left at game eye level. Flat opaque pixel clusters and a truly transparent alpha background, no base, shadow, grid, lettering, symbols, afterimages, glow or decorative effects. Keep each complete sprite inside its assigned square with empty gutters and a consistent ground baseline. Frame order: row 1 ready with shield held; anticipation draw shield back; stepping approach; shield thrust left with a lower left facing edge. Row 2 follow-through; re-cock shield; second shield strike with a different stance; lower shield and look back to step behind the next ally. Row 3 light recoil with shield; heavy backward recoil; one knee down; collapsed on the floor with shield tilted. Row 4 first walk step; second walk step; compact rear-rank ready; compact rear-rank anticipation. One consistent 4×4 grid. No text.

### 지원

> One production 2D video game sprite sheet, 4 columns × 4 rows of separate 48×48 logical pixel frames, on genuinely transparent alpha. The attached escort-idle.png is a pixel size, limited palette and light reference only. Design a NEW tower prisoner who supports allies and can fight directly, a small thin human courier in a frayed charcoal hood and swept short gray mantle, one rust-red clasp and desaturated gray wrappings, with a short iron hooked baton and one uncovered gesturing hand. The battered cloth surface and slight stoop should contrast with a broad stone shield sentry. The hood shows only a slight dull pale edge of a human face. Every frame of the same character facing left, at game eye level. Pixel clusters have sharp square edges, flat limited colors and a charcoal outline. No fades, magic bolts, particles, gradient, shadow, lettering, icons, props, ground, borders, or grid visible. Keep whole body and weapon fully inside its own cell with clear gutters and regular foot baseline. Row 1 idle; duck with baton in anticipation; approach step; baton contact extended to the left. Row 2 follow-through; second wind-up; short second attack contact; free hand stretched toward an ally to the left with fingers open and baton held back. Row 3 light recoil; heavy recoil; knee down; collapsed. Row 4 two genuinely different walking steps; compact rear idle; compact rear anticipation. Transparent alpha around the entire silhouette, no words.

### 보스 기본

> Create a new 2D hand-painted pixel sprite sheet for the final boss of a winter ruin tower game. The attached boss-idle.png is a style reference only for native pixel cluster weight, warm weathered gray and charcoal outline. NEW character: a massive former prisoner growing into a stone-caked executioner's mantle, broad gray shoulders and a broad hood with one canted grille aperture, asymmetrical fractured manacle still on the right wrist, and a long blocky executioner's maul with a chipped square head bound with thin oxidized rust-red iron. The maul is a solid tool and the character never has a crown or shield. Palette about 8 cool stone gray tones, near-black charcoal, restrained oxidized red, and pale warm snow-light for a few planes. Strong distinct silhouette, flat clean block clusters as if designed at 80×80 native pixel resolution. Every pose faces left and is game side-view. Preserve clothing, silhouette and size across poses. Lay out exactly 4 columns × 4 rows, 16 equal square cells. One separate complete figure with generous alpha gutters per cell at consistent baseline. Row 1 idle with maul lowered; light swing anticipation; step in; light attack contact with the maul head extended left. Row 2 light swing follow-through; re-cock for a short second attack; second maul contact; a guarded rest stance. Row 3 light recoil; heavy recoil; knocked to one knee; collapsed toward the floor with maul. Row 4 two distinct advancing steps; compact back-row idle; compact back-row warning with maul slightly up. No lettering, grid lines, environmental elements, floor, checkerboard, outlines around tiles, glows or attack effects. Actual transparent alpha background.

### 보스 특수

> Create a special attack extension sprite sheet for exactly the new character depicted in the FIRST attached sheet. Second attached tiny idle is only a pixel art style reference. Maintain the first sheet character exactly: former prisoner with broad grey shoulders, torn stone-like mantle, deep canted black grille hood, broken chain on right wrist, long haft and chipped square maul head bound with two subdued rusty-red bands. Clearly visible maul, chunky gray charcoal outlines, grayscale warm highlights and the same hood and proportions. Side profile facing LEFT on every pose. Native 80×80 logical game pixels per sprite. Crisp opaque square pixel graphics and genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside each individual sprite. No checkerboard, text, grid, shadows, fx or background.
>
> Arrange exactly TWELVE poses in a very regular 4 columns × 3 rows grid, generous gutters horizontally and vertically (keep every hammer head fully inside its cell). Feet anchored to the same baseline. Row 1 left to right: wind-up beginning with both arms lifting maul diagonally above head; full tall overhead hold, square hammer head silhouetted above head; attack step winding forward with hammer still up; full power contact, head of hammer reaches far to the left at waist height. Row 2: follow through with maul planted low left; weakened wind-up with maul halfway up; distinctly weaker contact with hammer low left, boss deeply bent; interrupted staggering with weapon sagging to one side. Row 3: interrupted braced kneel, grille face tilted down; recover with both hands leaning on maul; raised kneel as boss heaves the maul up; slow return to standing with maul lowered. The two overhead charge poses must be immediately recognizable by the maul held ABOVE the grille and not visually mistaken for a regular attack or idle. Draw exactly one complete character sprite per cell.
